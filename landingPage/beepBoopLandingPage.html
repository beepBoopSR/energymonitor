// The problem

Your electricity bill is a
mystery until it arrives.

Surinamese households pay bills they don't understand, for energy they didn't need to use — with no tool to see where the money goes.

⚡

Unreliable grid

EBS outages are a daily reality. Families fall back on diesel generators with zero visibility on what running them actually costs.

🔍

Zero visibility

No household knows which appliance eats the most power. The monthly bill arrives as a shock — never as a choice.

🌡️

Tropical energy drain

In Suriname's heat, AC and refrigeration run around the clock. Without monitoring, the waste is invisible and enormous.

// How it works

From clamp to insight
in four steps.

01 · SENSE

Clamp it on

Non-invasive current clamps wrap around your existing wiring. No cutting, no electrician, no risk.

02 · MEASURE

ESP32 reads everything

Voltage and current per circuit, converted to Watts and SRD using real EBS tariffs — every few seconds.

03 · ANALYSE

AI finds the waste

Our AI studies your usage patterns, predicts your bill, and spots the habits costing you money.

04 · ACT

You save

Clear advice in your language, budget warnings before you overspend, and outage alerts before the lights go out.

// Features

Everything on the dashboard.
Nothing in the dark.

📊

Real-time monitoring per circuit

Live Watts for every circuit group in your home — updated continuously on the web dashboard.

💰

Cost in SRD, not kWh

Every reading converted using real EBS tariff rates. See what today cost you — and what this month will cost before the bill exists.

🤖

AI insights & tips

Our AI analyses your patterns and tells you — in plain language — exactly what to change and how much you'll save.

🎯

Budget mode

Set a monthly budget — for the whole house or per room. The AI warns you before you go over, not after.

🔔

Outage preparation

energyLink tracks EBS announcements and warns you before scheduled outages — so you're ready, not surprised.

📈

Bill prediction

Know your end-of-month bill by day 10 — while there's still time to change it. No more bill-day shock.
